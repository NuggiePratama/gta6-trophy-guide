Leonida Trophy Guide is an offline guide and tracker for all 55 Grand Theft Auto VI trophies on PlayStation 5: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver and 46 bronze. Every trophy is shown with its official name, description and grade exactly as PlayStation publishes them, plus a guide written in this project's own words that cites its sources (the PlayStation official description and the Exophase and PSNProfiles trophy lists). A checklist saves your progress in the browser with export and import, filters cover story, missable, collectibles, activities, side, crime and economy, and a spoiler setting hides story trophies until you earn them. A five-stage roadmap orders the hunt — story first with every missable trophy flagged, then stranger strands, activities, collectibles, and endgame cleanup to the platinum.

Everything runs offline in the browser — no network requests, no accounts, no data leaves the machine.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080. Tick trophies off as you earn them, open any trophy to read its guide and sources, and use the Roadmap tab to plan the road to the platinum.
