Grand Theft Auto VI · all 55 PlayStation 5 trophies · unofficial fan guide

0 of 550%

28 of the 55 trophies are hidden on PlayStation until earned. This guide lists them all — turn Spoilers on to hide story trophies until you earn them.

Online: none — all 55 trophies are single-player. There are no online trophies in the official list.

    Earn the trophies in this order. Stage 1 is the story — do every missable trophy there before the final mission, because story moments do not come back. Stages 2–4 are free-roam work you can do in any order, and Stage 5 is the endgame cleanup that ends with the platinum.

    Sources & method

    Every trophy name, description and grade in this guide is shown exactly as PlayStation publishes it, from the official 55-trophy list for Grand Theft Auto VI on PlayStation 5 (list dated 2026-11-18). The counts PlayStation tracks — such as 42 Shooting Range challenges, 14 Top Fun drone race locations, 18 meditation spots, 52 areas of Leonida, 9 gangs and 8 garage properties — come from the same listing.

    Each guide is written in this guide’s own words. It draws on the official descriptions and on the trophy listings of Exophase and PSNProfiles, which agree on every name, description and grade, and it names those sources beside every trophy. No guide text is copied from another guide, wiki, video or forum post.

    Missable flags and the roadmap stages are built from the official descriptions: trophies tied to a story chapter or a named mission, and trophies tied to a one-shot moment (a partner robbery, an undetected theft, a choice), are flagged missable and placed in Stage 1. Everything else can be earned in free roam.
